The team that ships also operates.

Gohil Tech Empire is a holding company organised around one engineering practice. Architecture, implementation, and release stay with the same people — then those people keep the software running.

Client engagements and our own products go through that practice: AI agents, applications, SaaS, and the technical writing that documents the systems. There is no separate delivery team and no separate operations team.

The company is based in Kuala Lumpur. Work is staffed from here and delivered wherever the product needs to run.
